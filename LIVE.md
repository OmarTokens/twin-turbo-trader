# 📒 Twin Turbo: full race log

A live, public experiment: can a Claude agent following fixed momentum rules beat the market? Real account, small size, **percentages only**. Updated every Friday after the US close.

## 🧪 The test (set before the start; never changed)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | Mon Oct 5, 2026 (baseline: Fri Oct 2 close) |
| **Length** | 26 weeks |
| **Pass** | Beat **QQQ** after all costs at week 26 |
| **Context only** | **Bitcoin** (IBIT) and **SMH** (chip stocks) |
| **Quiet skill test** | The same 62 stocks held equally, with no trading: do the rules add anything over just owning the list? |
| **Expected range** | From the 2022–2026 backtest: 80% of 26-week stretches ended between −7% and +53%. A week outside the range gets flagged. |

**Honest prior:** in the backtest, Twin Turbo beat QQQ in only **51%** of 13-week stretches and lagged chip stocks (SMH) in 60% of them. Long stretches behind are normal. That's why the verdict waits for week 26.
